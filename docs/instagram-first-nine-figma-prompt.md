# Figma agent prompt: Rancher’s first nine Instagram posts

Create a polished, editable Figma file for Rancher’s first nine Instagram posts. The work must position Rancher as a trustworthy, commercially serious brand that understands business owners, respects work ethic, and treats business knowledge with care.

## Deliverables

Create these top-level Figma pages:

1. `00 — Cover & instructions`
2. `01 — Feed masters`
3. `02 — Carousel slides`
4. `03 — Reel storyboard`
5. `04 — Components`
6. `05 — Export-ready`

Create all feed assets at **1080 × 1350 px**. Keep every essential element within an inner safe area of **108 × 135 px from the left/right/top** and **202 px from the bottom**. Compose every cover so it also survives Instagram’s square profile-grid crop: keep its essential headline, logo, and focal subject inside the centered **1080 × 1080 px** region.

Create carousel slides at 1080 × 1350 px. Create the reel storyboard at 1080 × 1920 px, while keeping essential material within x = 108–864 and y = 288–1440.

Use Auto Layout, text styles, color styles or variables, reusable components, and named layers. Do not flatten text or logos. Place export-ready copies in `05 — Export-ready`, grouped and named by post number.

## Strategic direction

### Audience

Owners and operators of established businesses who have spent years building valuable processes, records, and expertise but do not yet think of that work as useful business data.

### Required audience takeaway

The knowledge their business earned through years of work deserves to be treated with respect.

### Brand role

Rancher helps businesses assess whether approved operational records could become useful licensed datasets for AI labs. Rancher helps assess fit, prepare and structure the data, apply appropriate privacy safeguards, evaluate suitable demand, and manage licensing.

### Brand controls

- The business defines the scope.
- The business approves the terms.
- The business retains ownership.
- Not every dataset qualifies.
- Do not imply a guaranteed buyer, payout, timeline, valuation, or revenue outcome.
- Do not claim that data is fully anonymous, automatically compliant, risk-free, or safe merely because identifiers were removed.

### Voice

Calm, practical, direct, respectful, and quietly optimistic. Use short, concrete sentences. Avoid hype, startup jargon, exclamation points, and exaggerated promises.

## Visual system

### Colors

Create Figma variables with these exact values:

- `Rancher / Ink`: `#193E34`
- `Rancher / Action hover`: `#285648`
- `Rancher / Muted`: `#536059`
- `Rancher / Paper`: `#F7F6EE`
- `Rancher / Surface`: `#FFFEF7`
- `Rancher / Line`: `#D9DFD3`
- `Rancher / Lime`: `#D8EE8A`
- `Rancher / Sage`: `#E0E8CD`
- `Rancher / Section`: `#EEF0E5`
- `Rancher / Focus`: `#658A2E`
- `Rancher / Warning surface`: `#F3E3D5`
- `Rancher / Warning text`: `#73563C`

Use Ink and Paper as the dominant pair. Reserve Lime for decisions, approvals, emphasis, and action. Do not create a neon or high-tech visual language.

### Typography

- Display: Georgia, regular; fallback Times New Roman.
- UI and supporting copy: Arial or Helvetica.
- Headlines: sentence case, restrained tracking, compact line height.
- Utility labels: uppercase Arial, small size, generous letter spacing.
- Never use all caps for the main headline.

Suggested feed scale:

- Display XL: 84–108 px, 0.94–1.02 line height
- Display L: 64–80 px, 1.0–1.08 line height
- Heading: 40–52 px
- Body: 28–34 px, 1.35–1.5 line height
- Utility: 18–22 px, 0.12em letter spacing

### Graphic language

Use editorial composition, generous space, thin rules, frames, checkmarks, bounded fields, document structures, process steps, and visible approvals. Photography should show observable work: hands, tools, records, equipment, facilities, annotated documents, schedules, or project archives.

Avoid:

- robots, glowing brains, circuit boards, holograms, or generic AI imagery;
- abstract data streams, floating dashboards, or decorative binary code;
- rustic nostalgia, cowboy imagery, cartoon farming, or cliché ranch motifs;
- fake customer records, readable personal information, or confidential documents;
- piles of cash, passive-income imagery, luxury signals, or exaggerated earnings;
- glossy stock-photo handshakes and staged boardroom portraits.

If suitable photography is not available, create clearly labeled image placeholders with specific art-direction notes. Never invent customer evidence.

### Logo

Use the supplied Rancher wordmark or logo where available. In the project repository, source artwork exists as:

- `public/images/rancher-wordmark.svg`
- `public/images/rancher-wordmark-white.svg`
- `public/images/rancher-logo.svg`
- `public/images/rancher-logo-white.svg`

Keep the logo modest and consistent. Preferred placement: bottom-left or top-left within the safe area. Do not distort, recolor arbitrarily, or add effects.

## Accessibility

- Maintain strong text/background contrast.
- Do not place essential text over visually noisy photography without a sufficient overlay.
- Keep body text large enough for mobile reading.
- Add an `Alt text` annotation beside every final asset.
- Do not encode meaning through color alone; pair status colors with labels, borders, or symbols.

## Alt-text requirements

Write concise, descriptive alt text for every asset. Describe visible subjects, actions, setting, and essential on-image copy. Do not repeat the entire caption or infer personal characteristics that are not visible.

## Final review checklist

Before considering the file complete, verify:

- All nine feed covers remain legible in the centered square profile crop.
- The final 3 × 3 grid has visual rhythm and does not resemble nine identical quote cards.
- Every carousel uses consistent progress indicators and slide numbering.
- No customer-identifying or confidential information appears in document imagery.
- No asset implies guaranteed qualification, buyers, revenue, valuation, compliance, anonymity, or risk elimination.
- All typography remains editable.
- All photography placeholders include actionable art-direction notes.
- Every export frame uses the exact requested size and naming convention.
- The supplied Rancher logo is used without distortion.
- The final file feels calm, practical, editorial, and respectful of real work.
