# Imagery

## Illustration style

Use restrained, geometric editorial illustration with flat botanical greens, warm neutrals, thin lines, and subtle depth. The canonical hero uses an isometric cultivated landscape to connect everyday business work with growth and value.

## Composition

- Keep backgrounds warm and quiet.
- Use simple shapes and a limited palette.
- Leave generous negative space for headlines.
- Prefer purposeful diagrams, records, fields, and transformation steps over decorative stock photography.

## Avoid

- Generic robots, circuit brains, glowing data streams, neon gradients, and sci-fi interfaces.
- Literal money imagery or exaggerated harvest metaphors.
- Photorealistic people presented as customers without evidence and releases.
