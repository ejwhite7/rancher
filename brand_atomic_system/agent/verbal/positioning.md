# Positioning

## Positioning statement

For established businesses whose teams create valuable operational knowledge every day, Rancher turns approved business data into licensed, privacy-safe datasets for AI labs. Unlike generic data brokers or do-it-yourself data sales, Rancher handles preparation, privacy safeguards, buyer fit, and licensing while the business controls scope, terms, and ownership.

## Category

Business data licensing and AI data partnerships.

## Promise

Make your business data work as hard as you do.

## Value proposition

Rancher helps companies create a new revenue opportunity from knowledge they already produce—conversations, decisions, workflows, records, and domain know-how—without asking them to become data companies.

## Brand principles

1. The business stays in control.
2. Existing knowledge can create new value.
3. Privacy and preparation are part of the product, not afterthoughts.
4. Commercial terms should be understandable and explicit.
5. AI progress can benefit the businesses whose work makes it possible.
