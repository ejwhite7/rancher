# Messaging

## Primary message

Make Your Business Data Work as Hard as You Do.

Your team creates valuable knowledge every day. Rancher helps you turn it into licensed datasets for AI labs—and a new revenue opportunity for your business.

## Supporting messages

### Value already exists

The useful raw material is already present in everyday work: conversations, decisions, records, processes, and domain know-how.

### Rancher does the heavy lifting

Rancher helps assess fit, prepare and structure the data, apply privacy safeguards, connect it with suitable demand, and manage licensing.

### The business remains in control

You define the scope. You approve the terms. You retain ownership.

### A practical path to AI participation

Rancher gives established businesses a controlled way to contribute useful data to AI progress and participate in the resulting value.

## Calls to action

Primary: Put your data to work.

Secondary: Estimate your opportunity.

Consultative: Tell us about your data.

## Proof language

Use concrete descriptions of process, controls, and outcomes. Do not promise a buyer, guaranteed revenue, a specific valuation, complete anonymization, legal compliance, or risk-free participation unless substantiated for that case.

## Boilerplate

Rancher helps businesses prepare and license approved, privacy-safe datasets for AI labs. Businesses define the scope, approve the terms, and retain ownership while Rancher manages the path from everyday operational knowledge to a licensable data product.
