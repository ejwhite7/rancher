# Claims, qualifiers, and legal language

## Required claim posture

Rancher can describe a possible licensing opportunity and a process for evaluating business records. It must not imply that every company or dataset qualifies, that a buyer exists, or that revenue will occur.

## Never claim

- guaranteed, passive, recurring, or effortless revenue;
- a guaranteed buyer, qualification, price, payout, or timeline;
- that data is fully anonymous, risk-free, automatically compliant, or safe merely because identifiers were removed;
- that Rancher owns a company’s data or can override third-party rights, consent, contracts, or law;
- unrestricted tool access or transfer before scope, rights, security, and terms are agreed.

## Required qualifiers

- Calculator: “A starting point for a conversation. Not a quote or guaranteed earnings.”
- Indicative scenarios: label currency, assumptions, and region; state that the result is not a valuation or quote.
- Fit: explain that relevance is assessed against buyer needs and that not every dataset qualifies.
- Privacy: say that de-identification reduces privacy risk but does not eliminate it; require legal and security review.
- Licensing: allowed uses, publication, redistribution, retention, exclusivity, and onward access belong in the signed agreement.

## Consent and forms

“Anything else to know?” is optional. All other visible partnership fields are required in the current flow. Describe outreach consent plainly and do not broaden it beyond data-licensing opportunities. Never place sensitive, personal, confidential, or production records in examples or ad creative.

Legal policy copy in `src/data/legal.ts` and the legal pages is canonical application content and requires appropriate review before substantive changes.
