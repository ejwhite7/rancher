# Copy taxonomy

- **Positioning line:** who Rancher helps, what business data can become, and how control is retained.
- **Headline:** one concise business outcome; editorial, sentence case, no hype.
- **Eyebrow:** 2–5 words, uppercase in display treatment, descriptive rather than clever.
- **Body:** mechanism and practical meaning in short paragraphs; define unfamiliar terms.
- **Primary CTA:** a specific next step such as “Explore my partnership” or “Submit & book a call.”
- **Secondary CTA:** lower-commitment navigation such as “See how it works.”
- **Qualifier:** immediately adjacent to the estimate or claim it limits; do not hide it in a footer.
- **Proof/trust note:** a concrete control or process fact, not an unsupported superlative.
- **Field label:** direct noun phrase; do not rely on placeholder as the label.
- **Help text:** what is expected and why, before submission.
- **Status/error:** what happened, whether entries remain, and the next action.
- **FAQ:** direct question answered first, then conditions and caveats.
- **Alt text:** communicate the image’s relevant purpose without repeating surrounding copy.
- **Metadata/social:** plain benefit-led summary; avoid claims stronger than the destination page.

Sentence case is standard for headings and actions. Uppercase is limited to visual eyebrows and compact metadata. Prefer “business data,” “licensed datasets,” “AI labs,” “define,” “approve,” and “retain” over metaphors or jargon.
