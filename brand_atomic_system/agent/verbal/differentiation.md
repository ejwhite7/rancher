# Differentiation

## What makes Rancher different

- **Built around the business:** The process starts with the company's boundaries, source material, and commercial goals—not a buyer's blanket request.
- **Control is explicit:** Scope and terms require approval, and ownership remains with the business.
- **Preparation is included:** Rancher addresses formatting, structure, privacy, and usability rather than brokering untouched files.
- **Designed for existing knowledge:** The opportunity comes from work the team already does, not from manufacturing speculative data.
- **Commercial and technical bridge:** Rancher connects business operators with AI data demand while translating requirements in both directions.

## True alternatives

- Leave the data unused.
- Build an internal data-commercialization function.
- Respond directly to one-off buyer requests.
- Work with a generic broker without preparation or domain context.
- Sell access through a broad marketplace.

## Boundaries

Rancher is not a promise of guaranteed licensing revenue, a substitute for legal advice, or permission to share data the business does not control. Avoid describing Rancher as merely a data broker; the preparation, privacy, and controlled partnership process are central.
