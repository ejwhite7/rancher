# Voice

## Character

Rancher is calm, practical, direct, and quietly optimistic. It explains a new category in familiar business language and treats privacy, ownership, and commercial control seriously.

## Writing rules

- Lead with the business outcome, then explain the mechanism.
- Prefer short, concrete sentences and familiar words.
- Use active voice and name the responsible party.
- Make controls explicit: define, approve, retain.
- Explain AI in terms of buyers, datasets, preparation, and use—not vague transformation.
- Use restrained confidence. Qualify estimates and future outcomes.
- Keep headings concise and editorial rather than promotional.

## Preferred language

- business data
- everyday knowledge
- licensed datasets
- AI labs
- new revenue opportunity
- define the scope
- approve the terms
- retain ownership
- privacy-safe preparation
- put your data to work

## Avoid

- unlock hidden gold
- monetize everything
- effortless passive income
- guaranteed revenue
- revolutionary or game-changing
- fully anonymous or risk-free
- your data is the new oil
- excessive AI jargon

## Mechanics

Use sentence case for headings and buttons. Use an em dash for a meaningful turn, not decoration. Use contractions naturally. Avoid exclamation points except in rare conversational contexts.
